# Ex-4.-Scenario-Based-Report-Development-Utilizing-Diverse-Prompting-Techniques
Objective: The goal of this experiment is to design and develop an AI-powered chatbot that can handle customer inquiries, provide support, and improve customer experience in a retail environment. Create prompts using various AI prompting techniques to guide your experiment, data collection, analysis, and report creation.
## Aim: 
To design and develop an AI-powered retail chatbot using diverse prompting techniques such as Role Prompting, Few-Shot Prompting, Chain-of-Thought Prompting, and Meta Prompting in order to handle customer inquiries, provide support services, improve response accuracy, and enhance customer experience in a retail environment.
## Algorithm: 
Step 1: Identity Initialization (Role-Based)
Establish the foundational persona by defining the AI's professional scope, tone, and brand boundaries to ensure it acts as a Senior Retail Liaison.
Step 2: Brand Voice Stabilization
Apply constraints to eliminate robotic language, replacing standard AI disclaimers with empathetic, solution-oriented phrases.
Step 3: Intent Patterning (Few-Shot Training)
Input "Query-to-Intent" pairs (e.g., tracking, exchanges, and returns) to prime the model for immediate categorization of customer needs.
Step 4: Classification Verification
Test the model's ability to accurately route unseen user inputs to the correct intent category based on the provided examples.
Step 5: Logic Protocol Structuring (CoT)
Establish a sequential verification protocol for complex tasks, requiring the AI to check purchase dates, item conditions, and policy exceptions before responding.
Step 6: Internal Reasoning Validation
Instruct the AI to process internal logic steps "out loud" to ensure it does not hallucinate or skip critical policy checks.
Step 7: Safety & Exception Integration (Meta-Level)
Implement a global override to handle high-frustration scenarios or ambiguous user goals that standard logic cannot resolve.
Step 8: Escalation Triggering
Program a specific "human-in-the-loop" trigger that activates if the user's intent remains unclear after two turns.
Step 9: Performance Stress-Testing
Run simulated interactions (e.g., 500 test cases) to collect data on intent accuracy, policy compliance, and conversation fluidity.
Step 10: Optimization via Distillation
Shorten and refine dense prompt instructions into high-impact keywords to reduce response latency while maintaining persona integrity.
